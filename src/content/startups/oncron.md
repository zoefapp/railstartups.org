Oncron provides an AI-powered software that helps infrastructure managers and train operators optimize train movements in real time. It gives humans decision support well beyond their horizon of visibility - from stations to depots to national networks. Where data allows, Oncron works from simple station-based inputs up to GPS-level precision, propagating the entire Belgian network hours into the future in under 9 seconds. It identifies, quantifies, and visualizes delay chains, highlighting recurring patterns that consistently disrupt punctuality and suggests alternative decisions.

The Impact:
- 5–10% fewer total delays → saves millions in delay costs
- 2–5% more capacity → new revenue opportunities
- Faster, automated delay attribution → less manual investigation effort

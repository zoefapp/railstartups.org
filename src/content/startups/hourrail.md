HOURRAIL ! is the go-to media for low-carbon travel! A community-driven platform built to make train travel and flight-free adventures easier, more inspiring, and truly accessible.

Through our website, our curated Premium app, our social media channels (700,000+ engaged followers), and our book, we cover everything from planning your first international rail trip to discovering slow travel itineraries across Europe and beyond. Whatever the format, our mission stays the same: give you the tools and the inspiration to travel without flying and to imagine a world where that's the obvious choice.

Founded in Paris, HOURRAIL ! is also home to Club HOURRAIL !, a membership community for travellers who want exclusive deals, expert tips, and a like-minded network of people redefining what a great trip looks like.
